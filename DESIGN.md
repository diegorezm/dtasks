# Design

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Visual System

DTasks uses a lagoon workspace identity: deep sea ink for copy, foam and sand for open surfaces, and lagoon/palm accents for live work and clear progress. Manrope carries interface copy; Fraunces appears only in large editorial moments. Panels are translucent, softly elevated islands with fine sea-ink borders and restrained shadows. Background light is layered water and atmosphere, not decorative chrome.

## Interaction

Controls use lagoon fills or quiet outlined surfaces. Hover states lift slightly or reveal a fine lagoon rule. Motion is brief, upward, and calm; support reduced motion. Desktop composition can place app evidence beside editorial copy. Mobile stacks evidence below copy without hiding the action.

## Avoid

Do not use generic dashboard grids as marketing structure. Do not make unsupported commercial claims, metrics, testimonials, or pricing promises. Do not use glass effects without functional separation.
